# Strictly Medical

A co-op isometric dungeon game about the Order of the Ninety and Nine, the first guild of medic knights. They go into dungeons to carry out the fallen, and they are absolutely not conquering anything.

**Play:** https://bondservant-of-christ.github.io/strictly-medical/

## What's in this build
- Lanternhold, a village on a floating island: Captain Maren (main quest and party setup), Odo the smith (sell ore, forge upgrades), Sister Ilse (flasks and stabilizing gear) and Tobin the miller (side quest).
- The Ashen Barrow, floor 1 of 3: enemies, copper and iron ore, chests, three wounded knights to stabilize and carry out, and the Lantern Warden guarding the exit portal.
- Real-time movement on the isometric grid, three weapon styles (sword, bow, lantern staff), 2 to 4 party members (AI companions stand in for friends for now) and three failure rules to try.

## Controls
WASD move along the grid · J strike · L skill · K dodge · E use (hold to stabilize, revive, extract) · Q flask. On phones: drag the left side to move and use the on-screen buttons.

## Running locally
Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server`) and visit `http://localhost:8000`.
